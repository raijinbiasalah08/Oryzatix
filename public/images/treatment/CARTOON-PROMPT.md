# Cartoon treatment artwork

Created with the built-in image generation tool. Each original disease image was used as its edit reference. The active card assets are the six `*-cartoon.png` files in this directory, with transparent alpha backgrounds.

## Edit prompt used for each image

Use case: style-transfer. Edit the supplied rice illustration into a clean friendly 2D cartoon UI illustration. Preserve the specific disease markings, leaf colors and rice anatomy from this reference. Bold smooth dark green outlines, simplified large shapes, flat vibrant colors with just two-tone cel shading; no realistic texture or fine hatching. Keep the entire specimen within the square canvas with a small transparent margin, centered and readable at 50px. Remove the entire white/ivory background: output true transparent alpha around and between the leaves. No white matte, no white sticker border, no background shape, no shadow, no checkerboard drawn into image. No text, face, eyes, limbs, labels or watermark. Only the cartoon rice specimen.
